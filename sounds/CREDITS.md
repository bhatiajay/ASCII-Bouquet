Each clip was trimmed, high-passed at 200 Hz to remove rumble (`meow-2.wav` was also
low-passed at 7 kHz to reduce hiss), faded in and out, level-matched, and saved as
24 kHz mono 16-bit WAV.

To use your own cat, replace these files with your own recordings, keeping the same names.
Short, clean clips of about a second work best.
